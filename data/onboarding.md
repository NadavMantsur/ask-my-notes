# New hire onboarding

Welcome to Lumen. This page is the first-week checklist for everyone who sits at **14 Harbor Lane, 4th floor**. Print it, stick it on your laptop lid, and check items off as you go.

## Day one

Collect your laptop from IT at the Harbor Lane lobby desk. They will have already created your Google account. Sign into Slack before lunch — the new-hire channel is **#arrivals**. That is the only place people post “I’m new, who do I ask about X?” questions, so mute it after week two.

Your onboarding buddy will meet you at 9:30 by the kitchen. They walk you through badge access, the printer, and which bathrooms actually have paper. They will also point you at the **wifi-and-office** wiki page for network details. **Do not guess the Wi-Fi password from memory or from a Slack DM.** The password is documented in that office page and nowhere else, because we rotate it when someone leaves.

## Your first product assignment

Unless your offer letter says otherwise, you are joining **Project Atlas** — the original billing platform, not the v2 rewrite. Atlas still runs production invoices. The v2 team sits across the hall and has a separate wiki. Mixing the two codebases is the most common first-month mistake; read both Atlas pages before you open a pull request.

First standup is **Tuesday 10:00** in the Atlas war room (4B). Bring a notebook. We still write tickets on paper for the first week so you learn the ritual before the tooling.

## Week-one checklist

- Slack: #arrivals, #atlas, #office
- Calendar: accept the Tuesday standup and Friday demo
- Docs: this page, wifi-and-office, project-atlas
- People: say hello to Maya Chen (Atlas lead) even if your manager is someone else

If something on this list is wrong, edit the page. Onboarding docs that rot are worse than no docs.
