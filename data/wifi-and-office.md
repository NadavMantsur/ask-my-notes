# Wi-Fi and office basics

This is the Harbor Lane office page. Desk maps, network, printers, and the Atlas war room. If you are a new hire, finish onboarding.md first, then come here for the practical stuff.

## Network

The staff network SSID is **Lumen-Office**. The office Wi-Fi password is **orchid-42**. Put it in your password manager; do not paste it into Slack. Guests use the captive-portal network **Lumen-Guest** at the front desk — that password is printed on the reception card and is different from the staff password.

If the staff network drops, reboot the Unifi box in the IT closet (badge required). Do not factory-reset it. After a password rotation we will update **this page only**. Other wiki pages may say “see wifi-and-office” and must not duplicate the secret.

## Printers and kitchen

The floor printer is by the east windows. The printer code is **4419**. Duplex is on by default. Color prints over 20 pages need a Slack thumbs-up in #office so we do not empty the toner mid-week.

Kitchen: coffee is on a honor-jar, milk is labeled by team, and the dishwasher runs at 17:00. Label leftovers or they vanish.
