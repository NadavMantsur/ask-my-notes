# Team rituals

How Lumen teams spend the calendar. This page is about meetings, not offices, products, or infrastructure. If you need a room number or a product launch date, you are on the wrong wiki page.

## Daily standup

Standup is **10:00** every weekday. Atlas uses room 4B; Atlas v2 uses 4C; everyone else joins from desks. Keep updates to yesterday / today / stuck. No live debugging in standup. If you are remote, post in the team channel before 10:05 rather than interrupting.

New hires: your first standup is called out on the onboarding page. After week one you just show up.

## Retro

Retrospective is every other **Thursday at 15:00**, rotating facilitator. We use the same board template: keep / drop / try. Action items that survive two retros get a ticket or get dropped. Atlas and Atlas v2 run separate retros; do not merge them because the names sound similar.

## Friday demo

Friday demo is **16:00**. Atlas and Atlas v2 teams **share this demo**. Original Atlas gets two minutes for maintenance status; v2 gets the longer slot. Other teams can book leftover time in #demo by Wednesday.

The demo Zoom link is **https://zoom.us/j/5550182**. That meeting ID is unique to this ritual. Do not recycle it for customer calls. Recordings land in the shared drive under `/demos/YYYY-MM-DD`.

## What this page is not

This is not a product spec and not an office guide. It will not tell you how to connect a laptop, which database to use, or when a billing system launched. Those facts live on the project and office pages so this file can stay short.
